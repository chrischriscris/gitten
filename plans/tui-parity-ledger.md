## Known gaps in this draft (coordinator spot-checks)

- **LG-030**: `files.discard` semantics assumed to act "by the side of the index the row sits on" from `act::discard_file` + section model; the untracked case (remove vs reset) was not behaviorally traced.
- **LG-005**: `files::Section::Conflicts` exists in code; whether a real conflicted repository renders it was not executed (W11).
- **LG-092**: `o`/`O` collision — upstream `o` (open/PR) vs gitten `project.switch`; the remap needs a product decision at W4/W10 integration.
- **LG-116**: EXCLUDED is provisional — the plan requires an explicit product decision for every exclusion; no other candidate exclusions were found in the pinned docs.
- **LG-105**: counting the layout registry as the functional equivalent of upstream's renderer cycling is a judgment call; confirm at W10.
- **LG-047 / LG-058**: both command families are fully wired (registered, bound, mode logic present) but refused by `tui_availability` — cheap early wins for W5/W6; the old `rebase_commands_remain_explicitly_deferred` test must be replaced, not extended.
- The status pane is reachable only via `ctrl-j`/`ctrl-k` (`status.focus` has no number binding — lazygit's `1` slot is deliberately empty); confirm that is still the intended slot policy.
